Background & Bio

Deep Technical Expertise in Mechanics & Computation

A career focused on rigorous physical modeling, high-consequence system validation, and pioneering the intersection of scientific computing and machine learning.

I am a data scientist and computational scientist with deep roots in mechanics, dynamics, and multiphysics systems modeling.

My doctoral research at Carnegie Mellon University investigated the intricate contact physics, friction phenomena, and surface mechanics of microelectromechanical systems (MEMS), characterizing dynamic friction transitions, surface roughness interactions, and environmental effects at micro and nano scales.

Following my graduate work at CMU, I joined Naval Nuclear Laboratory, tackling mission-critical engineering problems across structural, nuclear, and thermofuilds domains. I countinue to serve as an ajunct professor at CMU, teaching courses such as Feedback Control Systems.

"AI/ML modeling, such as physics-informed machine learning, allows us to achieve high-fidelity predictions in a fraction of traditional computational times."

Today, I provide specialized technical consulting, dynamic modeling, and research advisory services to organizations seeking to modernize their scientific modeling pipelines, accelerate numerical simulations, and model and validate complex systems.
